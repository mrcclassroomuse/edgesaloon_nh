Salon Etiquette & Policies

*All Stylists at The Edge Salon are Independent Contractors; therefore, The Edge Salon will not offer free services or refunds. That will be between you and the stylist.

1. Missed Appointments

A credit card is required to hold all appointments. A No Call/No Show fee will be collected for any appointments canceled less than 48 hours in advance and for missed appointments.

2. Late/Tardy Policy

We will always try our best to accommodate you if you’re running behind. However, tardiness affects our entire team. Clients are generally allowed a 15-minute grace period. After that time, we will call to check in.

If you cannot arrive in time for your entire service to be completed, you may have to forgo parts of the service or reschedule. A 30% no-show/late cancellation fee may apply at the stylist's discretion. Please always call if you think you might be late!

3. Guaranteed Services

We want you to be 100% satisfied. If you are not, please contact your stylist within 5 days of your appointment to schedule a correction. Issues reported after 5 days will be assessed on a case-by-case basis.

4. Children Safety

We prefer that children NOT accompany clients during services. Chemical services can take a long time, and we are unable to provide supervision. If you must bring your children, please provide something to occupy them during your visit.

5. Health & Safety

We maintain medical-grade sanitation. If you have any symptoms of illness, please reschedule without penalty.

THANK YOU FOR YOUR UNDERSTANDING

These policies allow us to provide the best service possible to all our clients. We very much appreciate your business!
